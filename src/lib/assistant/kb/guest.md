# Guest — PinkyBua FAQ

## Check-in
At the venue, scan the check-in QR and enter your phone number and the 4-digit code you received. You get a token number. If it says "ask the check-in desk", go to the desk; they can check you in.

## Waiting for the stage
Watch the venue TV. When your token appears under NOW CALLING, come to the stage. Your family pass also shows your place in the queue and refreshes on its own.

## RSVP
On your invitation page choose Attending or Not attending for each function and the number of guests (up to 10), then Save RSVP. You can change it later.

## Language
You can switch between English and Hindi at the top of your invitation or pass.
