# Finance — PinkyBua notes

## Shagun ledger
Add entries (cash, UPI or boxed gift) with a family or an envelope/token reference. Entries cannot be edited: to correct one, **Reverse** it and add a new entry. **Reconcile** links an unmatched envelope to a family. **Export CSV** honours the current filters and is audited.

## Summary cards
Cash total and UPI total are net of reversals; boxed gifts are counted; unmatched envelopes are live entries without a family.
