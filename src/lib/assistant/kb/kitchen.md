# Kitchen — PinkyBua notes

## Reading the kitchen counts
Each function card shows **Planned plates** (sum of guests for families who RSVP'd yes), **Attending families**, **Arrived families** (checked in) and **Arrived guests (est.)** (their RSVP headcount, or 1 if they did not RSVP).

## Meal status
Set **Not started**, **Serving** or **Closed** per function. The card shows who changed it last.

## What kitchen does not see
No phone numbers, no shagun, no private notes.
