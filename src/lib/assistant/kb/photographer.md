# Photographer — PinkyBua notes

## Uploading
Start a batch (function + camera name), choose files or a folder. Three files upload at a time with retries; if the page reloads, choose the same files again and already-uploaded ones are skipped.

## Clock calibration
With the same camera, photograph the QR on the stage console's **Clock calibration** panel and choose that photo. The offset between the camera clock and the stage clock is saved and applied to the batch.

## What "Check this" means
A photo is **Check this** when at least one stage call happened within ±3 seconds of its corrected time. A person must **Confirm** or **Reject**; nothing is matched automatically. **Matched** = confirmed; **Unmatched** = no call in the window. There is no face recognition.
